Signal construction and eligibility

Momentum = TR[t−21] / TR[t−252] − 1. Quality = annual net income / average beginning and ending assets. Average their cross-sectional percentile ranks; select the top {{ run.manifest.config.top_fraction|percent }}. Weight each selected asset equally, capped at {{ run.manifest.config.max_weight|percent }} of post-cost NAV. Unallocated capital remains cash. The liquidity filter is a 60-session median of raw close × raw share volume.

Availability, corporate actions and data integrity

Price history is eligible at the next exchange session at 08:00 New York time under the backfill policy. Filing facts wait one full subsequent processing session. Decisions occur at 09:00 and execute at the close. Later amendments do not overwrite earlier facts. Total-return adjusted prices include distributions once; no separate dividend cash is added. They imply frictionless reinvestment within each total-return asset.

Retrospective adjusted prices are not original historical vintages. Unresolved held-security marks stop the run. Explicit cash settlements and total losses are supported. Stock-for-stock transformations and complex corporate actions require upstream normalization and evidence. No live fill, market-impact, tax, or capacity model is claimed.

Metric definitions

Returns are decimal daily USD returns. Volatility uses sample standard deviation × √252; Sharpe uses the mean daily return / sample standard deviation × √252 with a zero risk-free rate. Sortino uses zero-target downside RMS over all sessions. CAGR uses elapsed calendar days plus the initial evaluation day, divided by 365.25. Maximum drawdown includes the initial capital peak; Calmar = CAGR / maximum drawdown. Undefined ratios are null.

Turnover is absolute traded notional / pretrade NAV. Half-turnover is also reported and excludes cash. Hit rate is the fraction of all daily returns strictly above zero. Returns and costs are reconciled daily; attribution is scaled by prior NAV / initial NAV before summing.
